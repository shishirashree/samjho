## gemini-3.6-flash · E3 Scam SMS pretending to be the power company · run 1

**WARNING: This message is a SCAM.** 

Thieves sent this message to scare you into giving them access to your bank account or money. 

Here are the direct answers to your questions:

* **What do I need to do?** **DO NOTHING.** Do **NOT** call the phone number. Do **NOT** pay any money. Most importantly, **NEVER share an OTP (One-Time Password)** with anyone. If you share an OTP, scammers can steal money directly from your bank account.
* **By when?** You do not need to do anything. Ignore, delete, and block the message immediately.
* **How much do I have to pay?** **$0.** Do not pay anything to the people who sent this message.

---

### Why is this a scam?
1. **The OTP Trap:** Real electricity companies will **never** ask you to read an OTP code over the phone to update a bill. 
2. **Bad Spelling and Grammar:** The message says "Costumer" instead of "Customer" and "was not update" instead of "was not updated." Real companies check their spelling.
3. **Fake Urgency:** Scammers threaten to turn off your power "tonight" so you panic and send money without thinking.

### What should you do if you are worried about your power bill?
If you want to check if your electricity bill is actually paid:
* **Do not use the phone number in the text message.**
* Look at a real, previous paper bill or log into your power company's official website.
* Call the official customer service number listed on your real bill.
